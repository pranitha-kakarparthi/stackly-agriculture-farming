Our Heritage

From A Single Organic Orchard To A Regional Agri-Tech Movement

Stackly Agriculture began with a critical mission: to rescue over-fertilized, depleted topsoils and establish a self-sustaining agricultural sanctuary in Tamil Nadu. By transitioning away from synthetic nitrogen and glyphosates, our founders proved that biological balance yields more resilient, nutrient-dense crops than chemical farming ever could.

Today, our certified cooperative cluster unites over 450 family farms with cutting-edge agronomical science, decentralized cold storage, and real-time farmer dashboards.

Living Soil Stewardship

We view soil as a living microbial organism. Through multispecies cover crops, vermicompost, and zero-tillage methods, we build deep humus and increase organic matter year on year.

Protected Horticulture

Climate-Controlled Greenhouses & Seedling Nurseries

To guard fragile young sprouts against erratic weather extremes, Stackly maintains 45 acres of high-tech ventilated greenhouse nurseries. Here, heirloom cultivars are germinated in certified coco-peat media under optimal photoperiod and humidity controls.

  • ✔ 100% Non-GMO heirloom genetics preserved for exceptional mineral and antioxidant profiles.
  • ✔ Natural biological pest management utilizing beneficial predatory insects instead of neurotoxic sprays.
  • ✔ Solar automated climate shading maintaining ideal photosynthesis temperatures year-round.
