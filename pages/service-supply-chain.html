Unbroken Cold Chain

Direct Produce Supply Chain & Logistics

Eliminating intermediaries to deliver farm-fresh organic produce to wholesale distribution hubs and retail networks within 24–48 hours of dawn harvest.

Farm-To-Hub Flow

Minimizing Post-Harvest Spoilage With Solar Pre-Cooling

Up to 30% of commercial agricultural crops in India rot before reaching retail shelves due to deficient cold chains. Stackly solved this challenge by situating decentralized micro-cold rooms directly on the field perimeter.

Produce gathered at daybreak enters 4°C solar chilling units within 45 minutes, halting cellular respiration and locking in natural moisture, sugars, and vitamins.

⚡ < 1.2% Spoilage

Industry-leading cold storage efficiency.

Live Fleet Telematics

Dedicated Reefer Transport With Live Temperature Telemetry

Our fleet of refrigerated trucks is equipped with GPS and live temperature sensors. Wholesale produce buyers can observe cargo conditions in real time directly from their buyer dashboard.

  • ✔ Instant Temperature Deviation Alerts: Automatic rerouting or cooling booster activation if temp swings >1.5°C.
  • ✔ Tamper-Evident Geofenced Locks: High-security digital seals opened only upon verified arrival at buyer destination.
  • ✔ Automated Invoicing & Gate Passes: Digital dispatch manifests signed electronically on delivery.
