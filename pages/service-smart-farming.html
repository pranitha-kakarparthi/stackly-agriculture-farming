Ground Intelligence

Subsoil Wireless Telemetry Across Three Root Strata

Stackly deploys solar-charged wireless probe clusters across agricultural parcels. Every 15 minutes, probes measure volumetric water content, electrical conductivity (salinity), and nitrogen-phosphorus-potassium ionic mobility across three root depth strata (15cm, 30cm, 60cm).

Data syncs seamlessly to the Farm Manager cloud dashboard, eliminating over-watering and allowing targeted biological fertigation right when plants demand it most.

99.8% Uptime

LoRaWAN solar mesh network functioning through monsoons.

38% Water Savings

Tensiometer automation delivering precise root drops.

Protected Agriculture

Microclimate Automation In Smart Greenhouses

For high-value horticultural varieties and heirloom seed nurseries, Stackly operates climate-controlled polycarbonate smart greenhouses powered by embedded sensor arrays.

Automated roof vents, fogging nozzles, and shade screens dynamically modulate solar radiation and vapor pressure deficit (VPD) to create idyllic photosynthetic conditions 365 days a year.

  • ✓ Closed-Loop VPD Control: Prevents calcium deficiency blossom-end rot in heirloom tomatoes.
  • ✓ Insect-Proof Positive Pressure: Keeps pest thrips and whiteflies out without chemical sprays.
Aerial Spectral Scouting

Multispectral Vegetation Indices & Stress Mapping

Early symptom detection across broadacre farm plots before visual human scouting can spot disease.

🛰️

NDVI Vegetation Vitality

Calibrated near-infrared reflectance reveals cellular chlorophyll vigor. Highlights nitrogen deficiencies up to 10 days before leaf discoloration occurs.

🌿

NDRE Chlorophyll Saturation

Red-edge band analysis penetrates dense mature grain canopies where standard NDVI saturates, providing accurate late-season grain fill metrics.

🌡️

CWSI Water Stress Thermal

Long-wave thermal infrared radiometry measures transpiration stomatal cooling, pinpointing clogged drip emitters and localized dry spots instantly.

Water Optimization

Closed-Loop Automated Drip Scheduling Algorithms

Traditional timer-based irrigation wastes millions of liters on humid days and starves crops during heat waves. Stackly’s agronomy engine combines real-time ET0 (Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration) with subsoil matric tension.
