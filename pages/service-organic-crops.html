Agronomy In Practice

Heirloom Grains, Pulses & Seasonal Superfoods

Unlike industrial monocultures dependent on synthetic fertilizers and pesticides, Stackly practices multi-tier companion planting. Leguminous cover crops fix atmospheric nitrogen into the soil, while deep-taproot varieties unlock dormant subsoil minerals.

Every harvest batch is cultivated under the stringent requirements of NPOP and USDA Organic standards, guaranteeing completely zero chemical residues.

Scientific Methodology

The Four-Stage Stackly Organic Crop Protocol

How we ensure pristine harvest purity from seed selection to warehouse dispatch.

1

Bio-Compost Priming

Soil beds receive aerobic thermophilic vermicompost enriched with Trichoderma and mycorrhizae.

2

Heirloom Sowing

Non-hybrid, non-patented heirloom seeds germinated with organic botanical seed treatments.

3

Botanical Pest Barriers

Neem-oil sprays, pheromone traps, and flowering marigold borders deter chewing pests naturally.

4

Cold Harvest Gathering

Hand-harvesting at dawn to preserve sugar and moisture levels before refrigerated lot boxing.

Ecological Regeneration

Measurable Soil Health Gains Over 5 Seasons

Industrial farming depletes soil carbon. Our regenerative organic protocols consistently deliver tangible, lab-verified biological enrichment:

  • ✔ Organic Carbon Boost: Increased soil organic matter from 0.4% to 2.8% on reclaimed acres.
  • ✔ Water Holding Capacity: Soil stores 42,000 additional gallons of rainwater per acre.
  • ✔ Microbial Activity: 4x higher fungal-to-bacterial biomass ratios for disease suppression.
